AI for plumbing companies: writing quotes and estimates

Short answerMost plumbing shops lose 10 to 15 hours a week to manual admin, and the ones who automate quoting, scheduling and reminders get 6 to 10 of those hours back. Start with quote follow-up, because unanswered estimates are the cheapest money you are leaving on the table. You do not need a big platform on day one.

How to do it

1

Start with quote follow-up, not the quote itself

Writing the estimate is not where you lose money. Not chasing it is. Automated quote follow-up sequences recover 20 to 30 percent of estimates that would otherwise go unanswered. If you do one thing, make it this.

2

Use AI to draft the line items, then check the math

AI is good at turning a job description into itemized quote language and estimate descriptions. It is not reliable at arithmetic. Let it build the structure and wording, then re-total it yourself before it goes out.

3

Turn on automatic appointment reminders

Automated SMS reminders cut no-show rates by roughly 70 percent. This is the least glamorous automation on the list and usually the fastest payback for a two-truck shop.

4

Pick a tool that matches your size, not your ambition

Solo operator: Jobber starts around $29 to $49/month. Crew of 2 to 5: Housecall Pro around $79/month. 5 to 20 techs: ServiceTitan, roughly $200 to $400/month. Standalone ChatGPT is about $20/user/month. Buying the big platform early is a common, expensive mistake.

5

Let AI handle the writing you keep putting off

Review responses, missed-call follow-ups, job-note summaries, and invoice explanations are all things AI drafts well. These are the tasks that quietly eat evenings, and none of them need a full platform.

6

Keep a human on anything customer-facing

Every credible guide says the same thing: AI drafts, a person approves before it reaches a customer. Especially on pricing and anything that sounds like a commitment.

Where this goes wrong

The parts most guides skip, and the ones that actually cost you money:

  • Buying ServiceTitan as a one-truck shop. At $200 to $400/month it is built for 5 to 20 technicians. A solo operator gets more from a $49 tool and an afternoon of setup.
  • Trusting AI arithmetic. It writes confident totals that are sometimes wrong. Re-add every quote before sending.
  • Automating the quote but not the follow-up. The estimate is not the bottleneck. The silence after it is.
  • Skipping reminders. No-shows are pure lost hours. Automated SMS reminders cut them by about 70 percent and take minutes to switch on.
  • Letting AI talk to customers unsupervised. Human oversight on customer-facing messages is the one rule every source agrees on.

Common questions

What is the single highest-payback automation for a plumbing business?

Quote follow-up. It recovers 20 to 30 percent of estimates that would otherwise go unanswered, which is money you already earned the right to.

How much should I expect to spend?

Roughly $20 to $100 a month for most small shops. Jobber starts around $29 to $49, Housecall Pro about $79, ChatGPT about $20 per user. ServiceTitan is $200+ and aimed at bigger crews.

How many hours does this actually save?

Independent plumbers lose 10 to 15 hours a week to manual tasks; those who automate scheduling, quotes and reminders typically recover 6 to 10 of those hours.

Can AI write the estimate by itself?

It can draft the line items and descriptions well. It cannot be trusted on the math or on final pricing. Draft with AI, verify and price yourself.
